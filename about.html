About Medirigg

Trusted Partner for Medical Equipment Solutions

Who We Are

Medirigg is a leading provider of biomedical engineering services dedicated to supporting hospitals, diagnostic centers, and healthcare organizations with high-quality medical equipment installation, preventive maintenance, corrective maintenance, calibration, and technical support.

Our experienced engineers ensure that every piece of equipment operates efficiently and complies with industry standards. We are committed to delivering reliable healthcare technology solutions that improve patient care and operational excellence. We are an Authorized Service Center for AGFA, MAQUET, Siemens and RESPIRONICS, delivering professional biomedical engineering services with experienced engineers and fast response times.

Vision

To become India's most trusted biomedical engineering service provider by delivering innovative, reliable, and customer-focused healthcare technology solutions.

Mission

Deliver quality installation, maintenance, and repair services while ensuring maximum uptime of critical medical equipment.

Values

Integrity, Innovation, Quality, Customer Satisfaction, Teamwork, and Continuous Improvement.
